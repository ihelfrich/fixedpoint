# ACCT 410x Study Platform

Comprehensive study platform for USC Marshall School of Business ACCT 410x - Foundations of Accounting.

## 🎯 Overview

This platform provides **3000+ practice problems**, **interactive simulations**, and **comprehensive study materials** to help students excel in ACCT 410x, particularly for **Midterm 1 (February 9, 2026)** covering Classes 1-7.

## 📚 Features

### 1. Practice Problems (3000+)
- **Balance Sheet** - Master the accounting equation and financial position
- **Income Statement** - Understand revenue, expenses, and profitability
- **Double-Entry Accounting** - Practice debits, credits, and journal entries
- **Accrual Accounting** - Apply revenue recognition and matching principles
- **Adjusting Entries** - All five types with detailed explanations
- **Accounts Receivable** - Analyze receivables and credit policies

### 2. Interactive Simulators
- **Journal Entry Simulator** - Build entries from transactions
- **T-Account Visualizer** - See debits and credits in action
- **Trial Balance Builder** - Create and verify trial balances
- **Adjusting Entry Trainer** - Master prepaid, accrued, and depreciation entries
- **Financial Statement Builder** - Generate complete statements

### 3. Adaptive Quiz System
- **Diagnostic Quiz** - Identify strengths and weaknesses
- **Topic Quizzes** - Focus practice on specific areas
- **Practice Exams** - Full-length timed midterms
- **Quick Quizzes** - 5-minute rapid reviews

### 4. Comprehensive Study Guide
- Complete coverage of all Midterm 1 topics
- Worked examples with step-by-step solutions
- Key formulas and concepts
- Common pitfalls and exam tips

### 5. Progress Tracking
- Track mastery by topic
- Monitor problems completed
- Identify weak areas
- Measure improvement over time

## 🚀 Getting Started

### For Students

1. Visit the platform at: `https://ihelfrich.github.io/fixedpoint/`
2. Start with the **Diagnostic Quiz** to assess your current knowledge
3. Review weak areas in the **Study Guide**
4. Practice with **topic-specific problems** organized by difficulty
5. Use **simulators** for hands-on practice
6. Take **practice exams** under timed conditions

