## 🎯 Ratio Categories Covered

### **1. Liquidity Ratios (Measuring Short-term Financial Health)**

**Ratios Included:**
- **Current Ratio** = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities
- **Quick Ratio (Acid-Test)** = (Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaid) ÷ Current Liabilities
- **Cash Ratio** = (Cash + Marketable Securities) ÷ Current Liabilities

**Learning Objectives:**
- Assess ability to pay short-term obligations
- Understand working capital management
- Analyze liquidity crisis scenarios
- Calculate refinancing needs

**Problems:** ap-ratio-e-001, e-004, e-008, e-009, m-001, m-007, m-010, h-004

---

### **2. Profitability Ratios (Measuring Earnings Performance)**

**Ratios Included:**
- **Gross Profit Margin** = (Gross Profit ÷ Net Sales) × 100
- **Operating Profit Margin** = (Operating Income ÷ Net Sales) × 100
- **Net Profit Margin** = (Net Income ÷ Revenue) × 100
- **Return on Assets (ROA)** = (Net Income ÷ Total Assets) × 100
- **Return on Equity (ROE)** = (Net Income ÷ Equity) × 100

**Learning Objectives:**
- Calculate multiple margin types
- Understand profitability decomposition
- Analyze return on investment metrics
- Compare profitability across scenarios

**Problems:** ap-ratio-e-003, e-012, e-013, e-014, e-015, m-003, m-004, m-006, m-008, m-013, h-001, h-006

---

### **3. Efficiency Ratios (Measuring Asset Utilization)**

**Ratios Included:**
- **Asset Turnover** = Revenue ÷ Average Total Assets
- **Accounts Receivable Turnover** = Credit Sales ÷ Average A/R
- **Days Sales in Receivables** = 365 ÷ A/R Turnover
- **Inventory Turnover** = COGS ÷ Average Inventory
- **Days in Inventory** = 365 ÷ Inventory Turnover
- **Operating Cycle** = Days in Inventory + Days in A/R
- **Cash Conversion Cycle** = Operating Cycle - Days Payable

**Learning Objectives:**
- Measure how efficiently assets generate revenue
- Calculate collection periods
- Analyze working capital cycles
- Understand cash conversion timing

**Problems:** ap-ratio-e-006, e-007, m-002, m-005, m-008, m-011, m-014, h-003, h-010

---

### **4. Leverage Ratios (Measuring Financial Risk)**

**Ratios Included:**
- **Debt-to-Equity Ratio** = Total Debt ÷ Total Equity
- **Debt Ratio** = Total Debt ÷ Total Assets
- **Equity Ratio** = Total Equity ÷ Total Assets
- **Times Interest Earned** = EBIT ÷ Interest Expense
- **Equity Multiplier** = Total Assets ÷ Total Equity

**Learning Objectives:**
- Assess capital structure
- Measure ability to service debt
- Analyze financial risk
- Understand leverage effects on ROE

**Problems:** ap-ratio-e-002, e-005, e-010, e-011, m-009, m-012, m-015, h-005, h-006, h-008

## 🔬 Ratio Formulas Reference

### **Liquidity Ratios:**
```
Current Ratio = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities
Quick Ratio = (Current Assets - Inventory - Prepaid) ÷ Current Liabilities
```

### **Profitability Ratios:**
```
Gross Profit Margin % = (Gross Profit ÷ Net Sales) × 100
Operating Margin % = (Operating Income ÷ Revenue) × 100
Net Profit Margin % = (Net Income ÷ Revenue) × 100
ROA % = (Net Income ÷ Average Total Assets) × 100
ROE % = (Net Income ÷ Average Equity) × 100
```

### **Efficiency Ratios:**
```
Asset Turnover = Revenue ÷ Average Total Assets
A/R Turnover = Net Credit Sales ÷ Average A/R
Days in A/R = 365 ÷ A/R Turnover
Inventory Turnover = COGS ÷ Average Inventory
Days in Inventory = 365 ÷ Inventory Turnover
Operating Cycle = Days in Inventory + Days in A/R
Cash Conversion Cycle = Operating Cycle - Days Payable
```

### **Leverage Ratios:**
```
Debt-to-Equity = Total Debt ÷ Total Equity
Debt Ratio = Total Debt ÷ Total Assets
Equity Ratio = Total Equity ÷ Total Assets
Times Interest Earned = EBIT ÷ Interest Expense
Equity Multiplier = Total Assets ÷ Total Equity
```

### **Advanced Formulas:**
```
DuPont ROE = Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
Sustainable Growth Rate = ROE × Retention Ratio
Retention Ratio = (Net Income - Dividends) ÷ Net Income
```
